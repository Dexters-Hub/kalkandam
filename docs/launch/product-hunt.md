## Maker's first comment

> Hi Product Hunt 👋
>
> I'm Alwin. Picking a Malayalam font has always been painful: fonts are scattered across old sites, half of them use legacy ML / FML encodings that turn into gibberish when you paste, and none of it works well on a phone.
>
> Kalkandam (കൽക്കണ്ടം, rock sugar) puts it all in one place:
>
> • Type once, see it in 700 fonts (Unicode, MLU, FML, ML-TT, ML-KV)
> • Legacy fonts just work: text is converted to each font's encoding automatically
> • Unicode ⇄ ML / FML converter for PageMaker and Photoshop folks
> • Manglish typing if your phone has no Malayalam keyboard
> • Export PNG or transparent PNG, or share straight to WhatsApp
> • Try your own font file, it never leaves your device
>
> It's free and open source. I'd love to hear which fonts are missing and what you'd use it for.
